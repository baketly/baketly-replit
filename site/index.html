Most bakers price by looking at the shop down the road.

Which is fine, until the flour goes up, or the day turns out to have cost more than it made. Three things a guess almost always misses.

Flour went up in March

You bought the sack at one price and are still pricing the loaf at another. Change the sack, and every recipe that uses it moves with it.

Your time is not free

Say what an hour of yours is worth, once. It lands in the price of everything you bake, including the proving you spent the morning on.

The stall fee disappears

A $40 table has to come out of the day's takings before a single coin of it is profit. Baketly counts it against the day.
